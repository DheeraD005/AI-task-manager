# ZenTime – AI-Powered Smart Task Planner

## Overview

ZenTime is an intelligent task management application designed to help users organize their daily activities more effectively. By combining AI-powered recommendations with personalized scheduling, the system creates optimized study or work plans based on task priority, available time, mood, energy levels, and productivity patterns. 

## Objectives

* Improve productivity through structured and efficient task organization.
* Reduce the effort required for manual planning and scheduling.
* Create personalized schedules that adapt to user preferences and current conditions.
* Ensure important tasks receive appropriate attention and priority.
* Minimize stress by providing a clear and manageable daily plan. 

## Key Features

* Smart prioritization of tasks according to urgency and importance.
* Dynamic schedule adjustments based on mood and energy levels.
* Automatic break allocation to maintain focus and prevent burnout.
* Support for multiple study or work time windows throughout the day.
* AI-generated recommendations for better time management.
* Personalized productivity insights and coaching.
* Emergency task rescheduling when unexpected interruptions occur.
* Focus tracking and performance analytics for continuous improvement.

## Proposed Solution

ZenTime uses artificial intelligence to simplify daily planning and task management. The system:

* Evaluates user-entered tasks.
* Determines task priority using contextual information.
* Generates an optimized schedule tailored to available time slots.
* Adapts recommendations according to mood, energy, and productivity patterns.
* Produces a structured daily plan that supports better decision-making and time utilization. 

## Scheduling Algorithm

1. Analyze all user tasks and their priorities.
2. Consider mood, energy level, and productivity preferences.
3. Break large tasks into smaller, manageable work sessions.
4. Allocate task segments across available study windows.
5. Insert suitable break periods between sessions.
6. Continuously adapt scheduling decisions based on user feedback and performance data.

## Advantages

* Enhances productivity through intelligent planning.
* Provides schedules tailored to individual preferences and conditions.

## Limitations

* Performance depends on accurate task and user-input data.
* Certain AI-driven features require internet connectivity.

## Conclusion

ZenTime delivers a practical and intelligent approach to daily task management. Through AI-assisted planning, adaptive scheduling, and productivity analytics, it enables users to manage their time more effectively, stay organized, and achieve their goals with greater consistency and reduced stress.
